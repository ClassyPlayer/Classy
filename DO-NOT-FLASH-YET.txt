FLASHING SAFETY CHECK

This build kit only creates a firmware archive. It does not back up the current player.

The ROM builder starts from a clean Y2 base ROM and replaces system components. It cannot preserve your existing Classy private database or playback progress by itself. A firmware flash can erase internal storage.

Before using Updater CE:
- Copy all audiobook/music files from the player to the PC.
- Make a separate backup of Classy's app-private playback state/bookmarks using a verified method for this exact device and app version.
- Keep a known-good Y2 recovery firmware and confirm how to restore it.
- Do not assume an Updater option called Keep Settings and Media also preserves Classy's private database.

If you cannot verify the Classy playback-state backup, stop before flashing.
